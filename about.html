Our mission

Safe rides. Smarter tracking. Peace of mind.

RouteX turns everyday school buses into connected, trackable vehicles. Live GPS, verified drivers and instant boarding confirmations put parents in the loop from the first bell to the last drop.

  • Every trip is mapped, timed and watched in real time.
  • Drivers pass three-tier verification before their first ride.
  • Boarding and drop confirmations reach parents instantly.

Our story

From one school to forty cities

2022 — The beginning

RouteX launches with GPS units fitted on 12 buses of a single school in Bengaluru.

2023 — Scale-up

Boarding & drop confirmations go live; first 10,000 rides recorded across three cities.

2024 — Every ride, tracked

Parent app and dashboard launch with live maps, receipts and ride logs for every family.

2026 — 40 cities strong

300+ buses, 12,000+ students and an operations command centre that never sleeps.
