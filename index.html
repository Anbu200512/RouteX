FAQ

Questions parents ask

Everything you need to know before your child's first ride with RouteX.

How do I track my child's bus?
Once your plan is active, open the RouteX dashboard — you'll see your bus's live position on the map, the estimated arrival at your stop, and boarding or drop confirmations the moment they happen.
Which areas and routes do you cover?
RouteX runs 14 routes across five pickup zones — North, South, East, West and Central — covering neighbourhoods like Greenwood, Lakeview, Hillcrest, Sunrise, Maple, Riverdale and more. Check the Route coverage section to see which stop serves your home.
Are the drivers verified?
Yes. Every RouteX driver is background-verified, trained and rated by the families on their route. Vehicles are fitted with GPS and CCTV, with speed monitoring throughout the journey.
What happens if the bus is delayed?
You'll get a delay alert the moment the schedule slips, with a live updated arrival time. If your child will be late, you can also see why and plan accordingly — no more waiting at the stop wondering.
Can I change my route or plan later?
Absolutely. Standard and Premium plans include free schedule and route changes, and you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime through the dashboard with no hidden fees.
